Company Profile

Carlton Hill Station Farm is a registered agricultural enterprise operating on 12,500 hectares in the Cambridge Gulf region of Western Australia. We are a multi-generational family business that has evolved from traditional cattle station into a diversified farm offering cattle operations, fruit picking, and crop planting.

Our employment model is built on transparency, worker welfare, and long-term opportunity. We maintain a 4.7/5 worker satisfaction rating and retain 65% of our seasonal workforce year-over-year.
