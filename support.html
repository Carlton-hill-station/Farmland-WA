Carlton Hill Station Farm wants to ensure your safety:

All applications and submissions must be made only through our official website.

We do not call individuals to request applications, payments, or personal information.

📞 If you receive any phone call claiming to represent Carlton Hill Station Farm and asking you to submit information or make payments outside this website, please treat it as a scam.

For your safety, only trust communication made through this official website or from verified contacts of Carlton Hill Station Farm.

Thank you for helping us maintain a safe and transparent process. 🚜
