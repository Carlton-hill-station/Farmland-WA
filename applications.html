EMPLOYMENT CONFIRMATION – PHASE TWO

Congratulations on successfully completing Phase One of your application to join Carlton Hill Station Farm.

Following the completion of your initial application (review in progress...)! We are pleased to inform you that you have progressed to Phase Two of our employment process.

At this stage, you are required to complete Section Two of the Application Process, which involves submitting additional personal and employment-related information necessary for:

  • Final employment documentation — including tax declaration forms and superannuation details
  • Allocation of your official work commencement date — based on role availability and seasonal requirements
  • Assignment of staff accommodation (where applicable) — private cabins available for full-time and seasonal staff
  • Completion of onboarding records — including induction scheduling and safety training

Important Information Regarding Your Data

Please note that this stage requires the submission of sensitive and confidential information. We want to assure you that all data provided is:

  • Professionally encrypted using 256-bit AES encryption
  • Securely stored on Australian servers with ISO 27001 certification
  • Strictly confidential and accessible only to authorized HR personnel
  • Used solely for employment and administrative purposes in compliance with the Privacy Act 1988
Carlton Hill Station Farm follows a structured and compliant recruitment process to ensure transparency, authenticity, and data protection at every stage. We are a Fair Work accredited employer.

Next Steps: Kindly complete Section Two within 14 days to enable the issuance of your official work date and accommodation details. A member of our recruitment team will contact you within 2-3 business days with further instructions.

We look forward to welcoming you to our team and are excited about the possibility of you joining the Carlton Hill family.
