Welcome to Carlton Hill

A place where tradition meets sustainable farming and genuine care for people

We are a family-run agricultural farm located in Cambridge Gulf, Western Australia. Operating across livestock rearing, fruit picking, and crop planting — we offer meaningful opportunities for individuals ready to grow in a hardworking, respectful environment.

Our farm spans 12,500 hectares and employs over 150 workers annually. We maintain a 4.7★ worker satisfaction rating through fair treatment, comfortable accommodations, and performance-based incentives.
